# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include being respectful, welcoming differing
viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what benefits the
community. Harassment, insults, trolling, publication of private information,
and other unprofessional conduct are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may ban
participants for behavior they determine to violate this Code of Conduct.
Report concerns privately through the repository's security contact. Maintainers
will handle reports promptly and fairly.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
